Frequently asked

  • Where is my data? Your data stays on your iPhone. The only copies elsewhere are your own device or iCloud backups, like any app’s data, and files you export yourself. PromptLux has no server and no account, and the app has no network code of its own. From version 1.2 the app itself contacts one outside service only: the App Store, for purchases. See the privacy policy.
  • How do I enable the PromptLux keyboard? Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard › PromptLux. Then hold the globe key in a text field that allows custom keyboards and pick PromptLux.
  • Deck or list? In PromptLux, Settings › PromptLux keyboard › Keyboard style switches between the Prompt Deck (pads), a text list, and a list with pictures; a new library starts on the Prompt Deck. On the keyboard’s Deck, the chips next to Add switch between your decks; tap a pad to insert its prompt or text, hold it to see its name. From version 1.2 a deck holds either prompts or texts: prompt decks have a teal chip, text decks a blue one. A new library starts with three decks: Everyday, Studio and Short Messages. To reorder, rename or take a deck off the bar, use Manage decks on the Deck tab or in Settings.
  • How do I make a pad of my own text? In the app, tap the pencil key on the Deck tab. Draft Message opens: write your text (up to 300 characters), choose its category, collection and deck, pick a face for the pad and tap Save. The page stays open for the next text. On the keyboard, tap a free pad: type your text or tap Use selection, pick an icon and tap Save. Saving a pad from the keyboard needs Full Access. A pad inserts its text; you press send.
  • Why does the keyboard offer “Allow Full Access”? Full Access is optional. Without it the keyboard shows your decks, inserts pads, fills variables and drafts a reply to text you select. In version 1.2 it is used for three more things, each only when you tap: Add (saves text you selected to your library), Save in the keyboard’s fill panel (keeps a pad you wrote on the keyboard), and Paste in the Reply key’s panel (takes a message or screenshot you copied). Reading the chat with the Action Button may need it as well. With Full Access on or off, PromptLux does not transmit your prompts or messages: the keyboard has no network code at all, and the app none of its own. You can switch Full Access off at any time in Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › PromptLux.
  • Which languages does PromptLux speak? The app, its ready-made prompts and its ready texts are in English. The Reply key drafts for English and German messages and answers in the language of the message. For a message in another language it says so and inserts nothing.
  • How do I back up my vault? Settings › Backup & Transfer › Prepare export, then share the single PromptLux-Export.json file — you own it. It holds your prompts and texts, their versions, collections, variables, decks and settings. Pictures you attached (prompt previews, pad pictures, collection backgrounds) stay on the device and are not part of the file. Bring it onto any device running PromptLux with “Import from JSON…” in the same place.
  • Prompt Doctor and the other intelligence features are missing. They run on Apple Intelligence (iOS 26 on eligible devices) and appear only on devices and iOS versions that provide it. Settings › Intelligence shows what this device supports. The Reply key and Reword need it as well. The library, packs, Composer and the keyboard’s pads do not depend on it.

The Reply key (from version 1.2)

  • How does the Reply key work? Tap the Reply key on the PromptLux keyboard, between Space and Return, and hand it one message: tap Paste for a message or a screenshot of the chat that you copied (Paste shows only with Full Access on), select the message in the text field and tap Use selection, or press and hold the Action Button (see below). The answers fit the message: a Yes, a No and Later for a yes-or-no question or a request; one reply for news, thanks or a greeting; only Later when just you know the answer. Pick Friendly, Professional, Warm or Direct, short or longer. A tap on a draft inserts it into your text field. Read it; you press send. Reword, in the same panel, rewords text of your own that you selected.
  • Why does the Reply key not draft? Drafting needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence switched on; without it the panel says why, and the pads work as before. It drafts for English and German messages only. For a message it recognizes as sexual, abusive, threatening or about self-harm it drafts nothing and says: “I can’t help with this message. This one needs your own words. Nothing was inserted.”
  • What happens to the message I hand over? The message and the drafts stay on your iPhone: a screenshot is read with Apple’s text recognition, and Apple Intelligence drafts the answers. The app sends them to nobody. PromptLux holds them, and the lines it read from a screen, in memory only: until a draft goes into your text field, you close the panel or the keyboard leaves the screen, and a message for at most five minutes after you handed it over or picked it. The message is not added to your library. See the privacy policy.
  • How do I set up reading the chat with the Action Button? On an iPhone with an Action Button, open PromptLux › Settings › Reply key and switch on “Read the chat with the Action Button”. Then set it up once:
    1. Tap “Add the shortcut” and choose Shortcuts. There, tap “Add Shortcut”.
    2. Open Settings › Action Button on your iPhone, swipe to “Shortcut” and choose “PromptLux Read Chat”.
    Then, in a chat: tap the Reply key on the PromptLux keyboard, and press and hold the Action Button. The Shortcuts app takes a picture of the screen and hands it to PromptLux, which reads the messages on your iPhone and drafts an answer to the newest message of the other person, with the earlier ones as background. Your iPhone may ask your permission to hand the picture over; the screen flashes each time a picture is taken. If no Reply panel is waiting, nothing is read. PromptLux reads the picture in memory; it does not add the picture or the chat to your library. Reading the chat may need Full Access. Settings › Reply key shows these steps with the names your iPhone uses, and you can switch reading off there at any time.

Free and PromptLux Pro (from version 1.2)

  • What is free, and what does PromptLux Pro open? PromptLux is free to download. The free version holds the first prompt deck with its first 12 pads, the first text deck with its first 6 pads, 20 of the 36 ready-made prompts, 6 of the 12 ready texts, room for 10 prompts and 6 texts of your own, and 4 of the 9 looks. Pads beyond that stay on your decks, grayed out; nothing is removed. PromptLux Pro opens every deck and every pad, all ready-made prompts and texts, every pack, no limit for your own prompts and texts, and all nine looks. Take it as a monthly plan (new subscribers get the first week free) or as a one-time purchase. The App Store shows the price for your country. To see the offer, tap Settings › See PromptLux Pro, or tap a grayed-out pad in the app. If a plan ends, the free version applies again; what you made stays yours and still opens, copies, exports and deletes in the library.
  • I bought PromptLux before version 1.2. Do I have to pay again? No. If you bought PromptLux when it was a paid app, you keep everything and need to do nothing. The app recognizes it on its own: from a library that was already on your iPhone before version 1.2, or from the App Store record that shows your first download was a paid version. The Pro page then says “You bought PromptLux — everything is included, for good.” After a reinstall or on another iPhone, if the app shows the free version, tap Restore purchases (see below) with the Apple Account you bought PromptLux with.
  • How do I restore a purchase or manage my monthly plan? Restore: in PromptLux, Settings › PromptLux Pro › Restore purchases; the Pro page has the same button. Monthly plan: while it runs, Settings › Your PromptLux Pro opens the Pro page with Manage subscription. You can also manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings. The plan renews automatically until you cancel it.
